## Quick compare tip

| Buy on Amazon / Harbor Freight | Buy on Taobao (usually cheaper) |
|--------------------------------|----------------------------------|
| Generator, vac, compressor | Tank, 12V pump, foam cannon, reel, bottles, towels, straps, plate |
| Anything 110V for US gen | Check **220V vs 110V** before ordering machines |

**Rough starter total:** ~$1,200–$2,200 US if buying mostly Amazon/HF.
