Zone 1 — Water system

Low-profile 100-gallon PE tank against the cab wall maximizes bed length for reels and tools. Strap into molded slots. Fill with DI water from a Blueshine-style spot-free station before jobs for spot-free rinses all day.

How a job flows from this truck

Same techniques from the research — now mapped to your GMC layout.

01

Park & power

Remote-start Champion. Drop tailgate. Unclip Coxreels.

02

Foam pre-wash

MJJC on Kränzle → thick foam → dwell → rinse from tank water.

03

Contact + DI rinse

Mitt wash, then spot-free DI rinse so you skip towel spotting.

04

Interior money

Pet tools → vacuum → Mytee extract on seats/carpet.

05

Polish / ceramic

SCANGRIP inspect → RUPES correct → coat with graphene/ceramic.
